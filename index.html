01About

I like to understand a thing at a fundamental level before I build on it, which makes me comfortable working alone. I value teams just as much: several minds together reach further than one.

Before my master's I spent three years training engineering teams and running demonstrations for industrial clients. It taught me that understanding a problem only counts if you can explain it plainly to the people who depend on it.

I would rather get something working and useful than chase a perfect version forever. I am based in Helsinki and open to relocating anywhere in Europe for the right role.

PROJECT 01Design for Manufacture
Full-Scale Nuclear Cutaway Models — PWR Reactor Pressure Vessel & Reactor Coolant Pump

Two 2.1 m sectioned training models for the Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission, reverse-engineered from an unscaled 2D illustration with no drawing set. Every internal component is visible, colour-coded and dimensionally faithful.

  • Established a reference dimension by hand and derived the complete 3D assembly from it in SolidWorks
  • Assigned each of several hundred parts to FDM printing, CNC machining or laser cutting by geometry, tolerance and cost
  • Split the shells into build-volume arc segments; bonded, filled and blended so joints do not show through paint
  • Managed revisions, BOM and manufacturing releases in SOLIDWORKS PDM through to crated delivery

PROJECT 02Industrial R&D
3D Scanning Feasibility Study — Vaisala R&D

Independent study on where 3D scanning creates value across an R&D organisation. Scoped with design, production, quality and test teams; delivered as case studies, repeatable workflows and a software recommendation.

  • Root-cause study on AUTOSONDE AS41 positioning faults: found manufacturing deviations up to ~15 mm across supplier-built assemblies
  • Scan-based drop-test evaluation: caught body-section shift and ~±1 mm deformation that visual inspection could not
  • Scan-to-CAD inspection of curved supplier tubes no hand tool could measure, giving evidence-based rejection reports
  • Scan-based jigs and fixtures for freeform parts that could not otherwise be held repeatably
  • Benchmarked four scan-to-CAD packages; recommendation adopted for future work

PROJECT 05M.Sc. Thesis
Solar Panel Deployment Mechanism — FORESAIL-2 6U CubeSat

Passive mechanism to deploy two 420 g solar panels, taken from requirements to a tested prototype. In orbit it gets one chance to open and no repairs, so the discipline was deciding what finished means before starting.

  • Defined requirements from mission parameters and CubeSat standards
  • Selected a dual tape-spring hinge through weighted concept evaluation
  • Built the prototype with workshop methods and off-the-shelf materials
  • Tested to 180° deployment, 50+ cycles without deformation, and full recovery after 30 days stowed

PROJECT 06B.Sc. Thesis
Prosthetic Socket Fabrication — Scan to Print

Digital workflow replacing manual socket fabrication: scan the residual limb, rebuild the socket in CAD, print, fit, iterate.

  • 3D scanned patient limb geometry as the basis for each socket
  • Reconstructed socket and component CAD from scan data
  • Printed, fitted on patients and iterated on design and print parameters
  • Estimated ~88% lower cost and ~90% shorter lead time than the conventional process
